About

I am a final-year civil engineering student at Shibaura Institute of Technology in Tokyo, working in the Mobility & Infrastructure Laboratory under Prof. Yiping Le. My research is in transport and urban mobility: how the public comes to accept autonomous vehicles, and how cities grow along their road networks, which I study with GIS and remote sensing. I graduate in March 2027 and am preparing to begin a master's programme in Japan from April 2027.

Research interests

  • Public acceptance of autonomous and shared mobility
  • Travel behaviour, survey design and discrete choice modelling
  • GIS and remote sensing of urban expansion
  • Transit, land use and transit-oriented development
  • Disaster risk and the mobility of vulnerable populations
